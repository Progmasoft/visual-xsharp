4. Defensive Termination

If a recipient initiates patent litigation alleging that Covered Software or a Progmasoft Contribution directly or contributorily infringes a patent, the patent licenses granted to that recipient under this Patent Grant terminate on the date the claim is filed.

If the triggering claim is voluntarily and permanently withdrawn, with prejudice where the applicable procedure permits, within 60 days after filing and before a final judgment on the merits, the patent license is automatically reinstated prospectively.
