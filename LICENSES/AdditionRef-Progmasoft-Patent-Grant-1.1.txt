"Covered Software" means the project-owned software expressly identified under Section 9 as covered by this Patent Grant. It excludes third-party material identified as such and software merely compatible or distributed with the Covered Software.

"You" means the individual or Legal Entity exercising rights under this Patent Grant. "Legal Entity" includes an entity and every entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with it. "Control" means direct or indirect power to direct management, or ownership of more than fifty percent of voting or beneficial interests.

Progmasoft "Controls" a patent claim when Progmasoft has authority to grant the license in Section 3 without a duty to pay a third party for that grant, other than compensation owed to Progmasoft personnel.

"Specified Combination" means only a particular combination that Progmasoft expressly designates by name and version, commit, release, or another unambiguous identifier in (a) the PATENTS notice accompanying Covered Software or (b) a public document that expressly states it is designating a "Specified Combination under the Progmasoft Patent Grant, Version 1.1." A general compatibility statement, example, test, API or ABI publication, protocol or file-format publication, SDK availability, interoperability claim, support statement, or distribution of software does not by itself create a Specified Combination.

"Essential Claim" means a claim of a patent that Progmasoft Controls and that, absent the license in Section 3, would necessarily be infringed by making, having made, using, offering to sell, selling, importing, or otherwise transferring:

(a) a Progmasoft Contribution as distributed by Progmasoft; or
(b) a Progmasoft Contribution as part of a Specified Combination.

A claim is not an Essential Claim to the extent infringement arises only from a recipient or third-party modification, from an element not supplied in a Progmasoft Contribution, or from a combination that is not a Specified Combination. A claim does not cease to be Essential merely because the Covered Software is distributed in modified, source, object, or executable form, if the same claim would necessarily be infringed by the unmodified Progmasoft Contribution included in that form.
